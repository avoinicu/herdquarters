Their `cloned_from` fields identify the original names and versions. The modifications change the layout geometry, rows, footer, and tabs. `themes/night-owl.json` adapts terminal colors from [Night Owl](https://github.com/sdras/night-owl-vscode-theme), copyright 2018 Sarah Drasner, under the MIT license. The panel colors are customized for this layout. A copy of the license is in [licenses/Night-Owl-MIT.txt](licenses/Night-Owl-MIT.txt).

Built-in Herdr mods and icons are referenced by path but are not included in this repository. They remain part of the installed Herdr app.
